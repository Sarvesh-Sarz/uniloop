Workflow

How CampusShare Works

A safe, 5-step peer-to-peer sharing lifecycle built exclusively for students.

1

Find an Item

Search textbooks, instruments, or tech tools available right on your campus with transparent pricing or free borrow.

2

Request a Rental

Select your dates and receive an instant quote with zero hidden fees. Owner reviews and approves your request.

3

Pick It Up

Coordinate a safe meeting point on campus—the Main Library, Engineering Block, or Student Center.

4

Return It

Return the resource on time and in good condition. The owner inspects it and your security deposit is released.

5

Build Trust Score

Earn positive peer reviews and boost your Campus Trust Score from 50 up to 100/100 for campus reputation.
